1. DNA & The Genetic Code

Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA) is the instruction manual for your body. It is shaped like a twisted ladder called a double helix and is stored tightly packed inside the nucleus of almost every cell.

The Base Pairs

The "rungs" of the DNA ladder are made of four chemicals that pair up in a very specific way. The order of these pairs creates the code that makes you, you.

  • Adenine (A) ALWAYS pairs with Thymine (T).
  • Guanine (G) ALWAYS pairs with Cytosine (C).

2. Dominant vs. Recessive Traits

You inherit two versions (alleles) of every gene—one from your biological mother and one from your biological father. How they interact determines what traits you actually show.

Dominant Allele (A)

The "stronger" trait. If you have even one dominant allele, this trait will show up. We write it as a capital letter.

Recessive Allele (a)

The "hidden" trait. It only shows up if you inherit TWO recessive alleles. We write it as a lowercase letter.

Genotype (The Code)

The actual combination of letters you inherit (e.g., AA, Aa, or aa).

Phenotype (The Physical)

What the trait actually looks like on the outside (e.g., Brown eyes, blue eyes).

Homozygous vs. Heterozygous:
Homozygous means the letters are the same (AA or aa).
Heterozygous means the letters are different (Aa).
