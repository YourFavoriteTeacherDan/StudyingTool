🔬 1. Scientific Inquiry & Design

Experimental Variables:
  • MV Manipulated (Independent): The ONE variable you change on purpose.
  • RV Responding (Dependent): What you measure/observe (the data).
  • CV Controlled (Constants): Everything kept identical for a fair test.
Hypothesis Format:
"If [MV changes], then [RV will do this] because [reason]."
Control vs. Experimental Group:
  • Control Group: Baseline group receiving 0 treatment (for comparison).
  • Experimental Group: The group receiving the MV treatment.

⚡ 2. Physics & Laws of Motion

Core Equations:
  • Speed / Velocity: v = d / t
  • Force (Newton's 2nd Law): F = m × a
  • Momentum: p = m × v
Newton's 3 Laws:
  • 1st (Inertia): Objects keep doing what they are doing unless acted on by an unbalanced force.
  • 2nd (F=ma): Acceleration depends on force applied and object's mass.
  • 3rd (Action/Reaction): Every force has an equal and opposite reaction force.
Thermal Transfer: Conduction (direct contact), Convection (fluid loops), Radiation (waves).

🧪 3. Chemistry & Reactions

Law of Conservation of Mass:
Mass of Reactants = Mass of Products
Matter cannot be created or destroyed in a reaction!
Chemical vs. Physical Changes:
  • Physical: Alters state/shape without changing chemical composition (e.g., melting ice).
  • Chemical: Breaks/forms bonds to make NEW substances (signs: gas, heat, precipitate, color change).
Atomic Structure: Protons (+) and Neutrons (0) in Nucleus; Electrons (-) orbiting in shells.

🧬 4. Biology & Genetics

Organelles:
  • Nucleus: Cell brain holding DNA.
  • Mitochondria: Powerhouse creating ATP energy.
  • Chloroplasts: Plant-only organelle for Photosynthesis.
Genetics Rules:
  • Genotype: Allele combination letters (e.g., Aa).
  • Phenotype: Physical trait shown (e.g., Brown eyes).
  • Dominant (A): Shows if 1+ copy present.
  • Recessive (a): Requires 2 copies to show (aa).

🪐 5. Earth & Space Science

Earth's Seasons: Caused by Earth's 23.5° axis tilt as it revolves around the Sun—NOT distance from the Sun!
Plate Tectonics:
  • Convergent: Plates collide (Mountains / Subduction trenches).
  • Divergent: Plates separate (Rift valleys / Mid-ocean ridges).
  • Transform: Plates slide past (Earthquakes).
Ecosystem Energy: 10% Rule (Only 10% of energy transfers up each trophic level; 90% lost as heat).
