1. Potential vs. Kinetic Energy

Energy is the ability to do work or cause change. In physical science, energy is constantly shifting back and forth between being stored up and being actively used.

🔋 Potential Energy (PE)

Stored energy based on an object's position or state. The higher you lift an object, the more gravitational potential energy it stores.

🏃 Kinetic Energy (KE)

The energy of motion. If an object is moving, it has kinetic energy. The faster it moves, or the heavier it is, the more KE it has.

The Law of Conservation of Energy: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, it can only change forms. When you drop a ball, its Potential Energy drops to zero, but its Kinetic Energy maxes out right before it hits the ground.

2. Thermal Energy & Heat Transfer

Thermal energy (heat) is just the kinetic energy of particles vibrating. Heat ALWAYS flows from a warmer object to a cooler object until both reach the same temperature (equilibrium).

Three Ways Heat Moves:

  • Conduction: Heat transfer through direct contact. (e.g., Touching a hot scooter motor, or a metal spoon getting hot in soup).
  • Convection: Heat transfer through fluids (liquids or gases). Hot air/water rises, cools down, and sinks in a continuous circular loop. (e.g., Boiling water, ocean currents).
  • Radiation: Heat transfer through electromagnetic waves. It doesn't need matter to travel through! (e.g., Feeling the heat of the Sun, or sitting near a campfire).

3. Introduction to Forces

A force is simply a push or a pull. Forces cause objects to speed up, slow down, or change direction. We measure force in Newtons (N).

⚖️ Balanced Forces

When equal forces push/pull in opposite directions. The net force is Zero, meaning the object's motion does not change. (If it's still, it stays still. If it's moving, it keeps moving at the same speed).

💥 Unbalanced Forces

When one force is stronger than the other. The net force is greater than zero, causing the object to accelerate in the direction of the stronger force.

Net Force = Total of all forces acting on an object
