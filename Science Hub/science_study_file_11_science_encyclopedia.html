Variables (MV, RV, CV)

The foundation of scientific experiments. Manipulated (MV) is what you change, Responding (RV) is what you measure, and Controlled (CV) stays constant.

MV = Cause | RV = Effect | CV = Constant

Atomic Structure

Atoms consist of a central nucleus containing positively charged protons and neutral neutrons, surrounded by negatively charged electrons in energy orbits.

Mass Number = Protons + Neutrons

Photosynthesis

Process where plants capture solar energy in chloroplasts to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose sugar and oxygen gas.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Newton's 2nd Law of Motion

The acceleration of an object depends directly on the net force applied and inversely on its mass.

Force (F) = Mass (m) × Acceleration (a)

DNA & Heredity

Deoxyribonucleic acid carries genetic code using four nitrogenous base pairs: Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Guanine (G), and Cytosine (C).

Base Pairs: A-T | G-C
